# Done-When Criteria per Stage

Every criterion names a metric, a tolerance, and a fixture. All four probes
(`probes.md`) ran in the pinned image on 2026-11-22; results are recorded in
`docs/results.md` (entry "next-stage-scoping") and folded into the criteria
below — tolerances marked *provisional* remain desk-set.

Fixture shorthand: `gcd.placed.def` is committed;
`picorv32.placed.def`/`picorv32.routed.def`/`picorv32.drc.rpt` are gitignored
and regenerated by `tests/designs/picorv32/generate_ref.sh` (~8 min, fails
loudly when absent, per `tests/designs/README.md`).

---

## E4 — Global routing

Reference: `global_route -guide_file` + `estimate_parasitics -global_routing`
on `gcd.placed.def`, then `picorv32.placed.def`. **P-GR measured**: the guide
format is `net` / `(` / `xlo ylo xhi yhi layer` / `)` per net (DBU rect +
layer name; li1/met1 pin-access stubs included — gcd: 269 nets, 1953 boxes:
li1 719, met1 867, met2 332, met3 32, met4 3);
`estimate_parasitics -global_routing -spef_file` exists and works;
`read_guides`/`write_guides` exist, and read-back guides are consumed by both
`estimate_parasitics -global_routing` (a 1-net guide file yields a 1-net SPEF
with the same per-net cap) and `detailed_route` (clean routed DEF, 0 DRC) —
the `GRT-0008` warning text is stale.

| # | Metric | Tolerance | Fixture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Per-net guide-box overlap: for each net, the share of OpenROAD guide boxes whose GCell rect intersects our guide for the same net | ≥ 0.70 (provisional — GRT is deterministic, so the bound is router-vs-router agreement, not run variance; tighten once measured) | `gcd.placed.def`, `picorv32.placed.def` |
| 2 | Guide wirelength: total guide-box count over all signal nets (gcd reference: 1953) | within 15% of OpenROAD's guide total, per-net compared not only in total (AGENTS.md §7.5) | same |
| 3 | Guide-parasitics agreement: (a) our RC model evaluated on OpenROAD's guide file vs `estimate_parasitics -global_routing -spef_file`; (b) our guide file → `read_guides` → `estimate_parasitics -global_routing` vs the same on OpenROAD's own guides — per-net total capacitance | within `max(5%, 1 fF)` per net (E3.3-style) | same |
| 4 | End-to-end STA on our guides → our parasitics → our `sta` vs OpenSTA on the `read_guides`-driven `-global_routing` SPEF: every endpoint's arrival and slack | `max(5 ps, 1%)` on gcd; picorv32 floors follow the existing routed_spef rows in `docs/status.md` | same |

Metric 3–4 are the load-bearing criteria (parasitics are what the STA
consumer sees); 1–2 are topology sanity that keep a degenerate guide from
passing by accident. Measured anchors (P-GR, gcd): guide-SPEF total
capacitance 0.7397 pF sits between the placement estimate (0.4406 pF) and
routed extraction (0.8962 pF); per-net total-C agreement of OpenROAD's own
guide SPEF vs the routed `gcd.rcx.spef` is median 27.6% / p90 111.6% (worst
cases are sub-fF nets), vs median 44.2% for the placement estimate — i.e.
guide parasitics halve the placement-estimate gap to routed truth, which is
the value this stage delivers.

## E5 — CTS

Reference: `clock_tree_synthesis -root_buf -buf_list -wire_unit` +
`repair_clock_nets` + `estimate_parasitics -placement` +
`report_clock_skew -setup/-hold` on `gcd.placed.def`/`gcd.sdc`. **P-CTS
measured** (sky130hd buffer list `-root_buf clkbuf_16 -buf_list "clkbuf_8
clkbuf_4 clkbuf_2" -wire_unit 20`): 5 `clkbuf_16` inserted + 3 dummy loads
(`clkbuf_4`×1, `clkbuf_8`×2), 35 sinks, path depth 2, avg sink wirelength
120.66 µm, clock-pin latency ≈ 0.31 ns, setup/hold skew ≈ 0 ps. Report
surface: `report_clock_skew -setup/-hold`, `worst_clock_skew
-setup/-hold`, `report_cts` (buffer/subnet/sink counts), `report_cts_config`;
`report_clock_tree` does **not** exist in this image.

| # | Metric | Tolerance | Fixture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clock skew (max − min propagated clock-network arrival at register clock pins, `report_clock_skew -setup` surface) | within `max(25 ps, 25%)` of TritonCTS on the same buffer list (reference on gcd is ~0 ps — the 25 ps floor is the operative bound) | `gcd.placed.def` + `gcd.sdc` |
| 2 | Max insertion delay (largest clock-pin arrival; gcd reference ≈ 0.31 ns) | within 15% of TritonCTS | same |
| 3 | End-to-end: `set_propagated_clock` STA on our CTS DEF vs OpenSTA propagated STA on the TritonCTS DEF — endpoint arrival/slack | `max(5 ps, 1%)` (E3 propagated tolerance) | same |
| 4 | Honesty gate: inserted buffer count (gcd reference: 5 buffers + 3 dummy loads) and clock-net wirelength reported next to TritonCTS's in `docs/results.md` (informational, not pass/fail — tree shape is legitimately free) | — | same |

`gcd` has one clock on 35 register sinks; `picorv32` is the scale-up fixture
once `gcd` passes.

## E6 — Detailed routing

Reference: `detailed_route -output_drc` + `write_def` + `extract_parasitics`
(step 5b, already reproducible on gcd and picorv32).

| # | Metric | Tolerance | Fixture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DRC violations in a reference check on our routed DEF — reference checker: Magic `drc style drc(full)` + `drc count total` on `def read` (P-DRC confirmed working on `gcd.routed.def`: 0 errors, matching the committed empty `gcd.drc.rpt`) | 0 violations on gcd | `gcd.placed.def` routed by us |
| 2 | Total signal wirelength + via count | within 20% / 25% of DRT's reported numbers on the same input (provisional) | same |
| 3 | `extract_parasitics` + `write_spef` on our routed DEF → OpenSTA vs our `sta` on that SPEF | E3.3 tolerance (`max(5 ps, 1%)`, design floors apply) | same |

Criterion 1 is the real bar; 2 is a quality bound; 3 measures the timing
consequence. **Recommendation: this stage is decision-gated, not scheduled**
(see `order.md`) — its missing surface (via/rule parsing, track router,
DRC-awareness) is an order of magnitude beyond any prior stage, and P-GR
confirmed the delegation path: `read_guides` + `detailed_route` on a foreign
guide file yields a routed DEF with 0 DRC on gcd, so our E4 guides can be
handed to OpenROAD's own DRT for a usable flow without writing a DRT.

## E7 — DRC

Only meaningful with routed shapes to check. **P-DRC measured**: the image
ships Magic 8.3.623 and KLayout; `magic -dnull -noconsole -rcfile
/pdk/sky130A/libs.tech/magic/sky130A.magicrc` (requires `-e PDK_ROOT=/pdk`)
with `lef read` (tlef + cell lef) + `def read gcd.routed.def` + `load` +
`select top cell` + `drc euclidean on` + `drc style drc(full)` + `drc check`
reports **0 errors** on `gcd.routed.def` via `drc count total`, per-rule
detail via `drc listall why`. The standalone scoping keeps its independent
reference. Two scopings:

- **Paired with E6** (check our own routes): criteria are E6 criterion 1 —
  per-rule violation counts matching the reference checker's on identical
  DEFs, per rule name, exact integer equality (AGENTS.md §7.5: integer
  quantities compare per element).
- **Standalone subset checker** (pre-E6 machinery builder): parse a chosen
  LEF-rule subset (min-width, same-net/diff-net spacing, via enclosure),
  evaluate on committed `gcd.routed.def` + `picorv32.routed.def`, and require
  per-rule violation counts equal to Magic's `drc listall why` breakdown on
  the same DEFs.

## E8 — IR drop

Reference: PDNSim inside OpenROAD — **P-IRD measured**: `analyze_power_grid`,
`check_power_grid`, `set_pdnsim_net_voltage`, `set_pdnsim_source_settings`,
`set_pdnsim_inst_power` all exist in the pinned image (no `*psm*`/`*irdrop*`
commands). On `gcd.placed.def` the power nets are `VPWR`/`VGND` (not VDD/VSS);
`analyze_power_grid -net VPWR` reports total power 1.04 mW, supply 1.80 V,
average IR drop 0.223 mV, **worst-case IR drop 0.591 mV** (0.03%), and
`-voltage_file` emits a per-instance CSV (`Instance,Terminal,Layer,X,Y,
Voltage` — one row per instance power terminal). `check_power_grid` verifies
PDN connectivity ("All shapes on net VPWR are connected").

| # | Metric | Tolerance | Fixture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Worst static IR drop on `VPWR` (`analyze_power_grid` report) | within `max(5%, 5 mV)` of the reference (gcd reference: 0.591 mV — the 5 mV floor dominates on this fixture) | `gcd.placed.def` (PDN in SPECIALNETS) |
| 2 | Per-instance voltage agreement against the `-voltage_file` CSV | per-instance absolute difference `max(5%, 1 mV)` (E3-style floor; denser than a rank correlation and the CSV gives exact positions) | same |
| 3 | Gate: net current estimate documented — what drives the reference's 1.04 mW total (embedded OpenSTA power estimate on default activities vs `set_pdnsim_inst_power` annotation) is pinned in the E8 golden ticket; our side uses N7 `estimate_power` per-instance numbers | — | same |

The stage is **schedulable**: the reference exists in the container.
