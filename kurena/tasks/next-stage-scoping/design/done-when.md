# Done-When Criteria per Stage

Every criterion names a metric, a tolerance, and a fixture. Where the
tolerance or the reference surface itself cannot be set from the desk, the
deciding probe is named (`probes.md`); its result is recorded in
`docs/results.md` and the criterion is tightened to the measured value.

Fixture shorthand: `gcd.placed.def` is committed;
`picorv32.placed.def`/`picorv32.routed.def`/`picorv32.drc.rpt` are gitignored
and regenerated by `tests/designs/picorv32/generate_ref.sh` (~8 min, fails
loudly when absent, per `tests/designs/README.md`).

---

## E4 — Global routing

Reference: `global_route -guide_file` + `estimate_parasitics -global_routing`
on `gcd.placed.def`, then `picorv32.placed.def`.

| # | Metric | Tolerance | Fixture | Probe dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Per-net guide-box overlap: for each net, the share of OpenROAD guide boxes whose GCell rect intersects our guide for the same net | ≥ threshold set by P-GR (desk estimate 0.70; tighten to what the reference's own run-to-run variance shows) | `gcd.placed.def`, `picorv32.placed.def` | P-GR dumps guide format + coverage math |
| 2 | Guide wirelength: total guide edge count over all signal nets | within 15% of OpenROAD's guide total (P-GR measures the real spread; integer quantities compare per-net, not only totals — AGENTS.md §7.5) | same | P-GR |
| 3 | Guide-parasitics agreement, isolated: our RC model evaluated on OpenROAD's own guides vs OpenROAD `estimate_parasitics -global_routing -spef_file` — per-net total capacitance | within `max(5%, 1 fF)` per net (E3.3-style); provisional — the probe first confirms `-global_routing` exists in the pinned image | same | P-GR |
| 4 | End-to-end STA on our guides → our parasitics → our `sta` vs OpenSTA on OpenROAD guides → OpenROAD `-global_routing` SPEF: every endpoint's arrival and slack | `max(5 ps, 1%)` on gcd; picorv32 floors follow the existing routed_spef rows in `docs/status.md` | same | P-GR |

Metric 3–4 are the load-bearing criteria (parasitics are what the STA
consumer sees); 1–2 are topology sanity that keep a degenerate guide from
passing by accident.

## E5 — CTS

Reference: `clock_tree_synthesis` + `repair_clock_nets` +
`estimate_parasitics -placement` + `report_clock_skew`/`report_clock_tree` on
`gcd.placed.def`/`gcd.sdc`.

| # | Metric | Tolerance | Fixture | Probe dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clock skew (max − min propagated clock-network arrival at register clock pins, `report_clock_skew -setup` surface) | within `max(25 ps, 25%)` of TritonCTS on the same buffer list — provisional; P-CTS measures the reference skew first (~sky130 gcd trees report tens to ~200 ps) | `gcd.placed.def` + `gcd.sdc` | P-CTS (reference magnitude + report command names) |
| 2 | Max insertion delay (largest clock-pin arrival) | within 15% of TritonCTS | same | P-CTS |
| 3 | End-to-end: `set_propagated_clock` STA on our CTS DEF vs OpenSTA propagated STA on the TritonCTS DEF — endpoint arrival/slack | `max(5 ps, 1%)` (E3 propagated tolerance) | same | P-CTS emits the TritonCTS DEF |
| 4 | Honesty gate: inserted buffer count and clock-net wirelength reported next to TritonCTS's in `docs/results.md` (informational, not pass/fail — tree shape is legitimately free) | — | same | — |

`gcd` has one clock on ~50 register pins; `picorv32` is the scale-up fixture
once `gcd` passes.

## E6 — Detailed routing

Reference: `detailed_route -output_drc` + `write_def` + `extract_parasitics`
(step 5b, already reproducible on gcd and picorv32).

| # | Metric | Tolerance | Fixture | Probe dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DRC violations in `detailed_route`-equivalent check on our routed DEF — reference checker: OpenROAD's own route-check surface if usable on foreign DEFs, else Magic `drc` | 0 violations on gcd | `gcd.placed.def` routed by us | P-DRC (which checker can check our DEF) |
| 2 | Total signal wirelength + via count | within 20% / 25% of DRT's reported numbers on the same input (provisional) | same | — |
| 3 | `extract_parasitics` + `write_spef` on our routed DEF → OpenSTA vs our `sta` on that SPEF | E3.3 tolerance (`max(5 ps, 1%)`, design floors apply) | same | — |

Criterion 1 is the real bar; 2 is a quality bound; 3 measures the timing
consequence. **Recommendation: this stage is decision-gated, not scheduled**
(see `order.md`) — its missing surface (via/rule parsing, track router,
DRC-awareness) is an order of magnitude beyond any prior stage, and E4's
guides already deliver most of the routed-timing signal at a fraction of the
cost.

## E7 — DRC

Only meaningful with routed shapes to check. Two scopings:

- **Paired with E6** (check our own routes): criteria are E6 criterion 1 —
  per-rule violation counts matching the reference checker's on identical
  DEFs, per rule name, exact integer equality (AGENTS.md §7.5: integer
  quantities compare per element).
- **Standalone subset checker** (pre-E6 machinery builder): parse a chosen
  LEF-rule subset (min-width, same-net/diff-net spacing, via enclosure),
  evaluate on committed `gcd.routed.def` + `picorv32.routed.def`, and require
  per-rule violation counts equal to the reference's. Reference: Magic `drc`
  per-rule counts if the image's magic can `def read` (P-DRC decides; Magic's
  sky130 deck is the established independent checker). If magic cannot read
  DEF in this image, the standalone scoping has no independent reference and
  drops out of the recommendation.

## E8 — IR drop

Reference: PDNSim inside OpenROAD (`analyze_power_grid` /
`check_power_grid`).

| # | Metric | Tolerance | Fixture | Probe dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Worst static IR drop on `VDD` | within `max(5%, 5 mV)` of the reference | `gcd.placed.def` (PDN in SPECIALNETS) | P-IRD (command exists? metric surface?) |
| 2 | Per-instance or grid-node voltage map agreement | E1-style map comparison (Spearman ρ ≥ 0.9 — provisional) | same | P-IRD |
| 3 | Gate: net current estimate documented — whether it uses N7 per-instance average power or leakage-only static mode, chosen to match what the reference drives | — | same | P-IRD |

**Gate on P-IRD**: if the pinned image does not ship PDNSim's commands,
there is no reference tool for this stage in the container (OpenSTA does not
do IR drop) and the stage is recorded as deferred-until-reference — the
discipline in `AGENTS.md` §2 does not allow a self-defined golden.
